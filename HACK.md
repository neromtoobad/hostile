# Idea

**One sentence:** a fantasy stock league where every pick is a real share bought onchain, and each week the loser hands 10% of their shares to the winner.

- **Similar products:**
  - Fantasy stock apps (Fantasy Stock League, TradeChamps) use paper points.
  - Crypto fantasy leagues (CFL on Solana) draft memecoins and settle in a prize pool.
  - **What's new here:**
    - The draft is a real purchase of Coinbase-issued stock tokens on Base, and you keep the shares.
    - The weekly result moves real shares between wallets, not points.
    - The rake buys stocks into a public treasury that the champion takes.
- **Why it needs the chain:**
  - The league contract holds the shares.
  - Scoring uses Aerodrome's onchain price history (TWAP), so no one, including us, sets the score.
  - The payout is a real stock-token transfer that anyone can see on BaseScan.
- **Useful with one user on day one:** create a league with friends. The draft alone gets you a real stock portfolio.
- **WOW:** the losing manager's NVDA lands in the winner's wallet, and BaseScan shows the transfer.
